Huh? Extension Test Sandbox

Use this page to verify all V1 MVP capabilities of your Huh? Chrome Extension.

1. Normal Highlight Lookups

Try highlighting the bold terms below and click the floating huh? trigger button to fetch their definitions:

We migrated our application cluster to Kubernetes to automate scaling and container management.

The CFO announced that our EBITDA increased by 15% year-over-year during the earnings call.

To reduce hallucination in our large language model, we implemented a RAG pipeline connected to our vector store.

Our developers are currently building custom integrations using the MCP (Model Context Protocol) to link AI agents with local shell utilities.

2. Context Disambiguation

The extension should capture the surrounding sentence to resolve ambiguity. Highlight the word Apple in both boxes below and see how the classification changes:

Context A (Company)

Yesterday, Apple reported record quarterly revenue driven by strong iPhone sales globally.

Context B (Fruit)

A fresh organic red Apple contains approximately 95 calories and is a rich source of dietary fiber.

3. Non-Selectable Text Fallback

The box below has CSS user-select: none; applied, meaning you cannot drag-to-highlight the text. Test the Alt + Click lookup fallback:

Hold down the Alt key on your keyboard and click directly on a word below. The explanation card should open immediately.

Hold Alt and click: Palantir specializes in big data analytics platforms. Or click here: ubiquitous computing is becoming a standard in modern smart homes.

4. Quick Verification Guide

  1. Click the extension icon in the toolbar to configure your API Provider, API Key, and Model. Click Save.
  2. Perform lookups on this page. Verify that:
    • The floating trigger button appears under 100ms.
    • Clicking the trigger displays a card with a Title, Category (e.g. Technology, Company), Subtitle, and 1-3 sentence explanation.
    • Clicking anywhere else dismisses the card automatically.
    • Repeating a lookup on the same term loads instantly with the "Instant Cache Hit" indicator.
